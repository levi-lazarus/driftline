FAQ

Quick answers.

Can't find it here? Send us a message — we actually read them.

How long does shipping take?

Lagos: 1–2 working days. Rest of Nigeria: 2–5 days. International: 5–10 days with full tracking. Orders over ₦150,000 ship free.

What's your returns policy?

Unworn items with tags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Size swaps are always free — we'll send the new size as soon as the original is on its way back.

How do your sizes run?

Sneakers are true to size in EU sizing. Hoodies and tees have a relaxed, boxy fit — size down for a regular fit. Jackets are true to size.

Do you restock sold-out drops?

Core pieces (tees, hoodies, Court Classic) restock regularly. Limited drops like the Heritage High '85 are one-and-done — join The Drift List for first access.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, within one hour of placing it. Message us with your order number and we'll sort it before it ships.
