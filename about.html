DRIFTLINE started in a two-room studio in Yaba with one sewing machine, a borrowed heat press and a simple idea: streetwear that feels like the city it comes from — loud, warm, restless and impossible to ignore.

The story

We never liked straight lines.

In 2019, our founders — a footwear designer and a photographer who met queuing for a sneaker release — got tired of choosing between hype pieces that fell apart and basics that said nothing. So they made their own.

The first drop was 40 hoodies, screen-printed by hand and sold out of the boot of a car on the Lekki–Ikoyi bridge. It sold out in nine minutes. The second drop sold out in four.

Today we design every piece in Lagos, work with small partner workshops across West Africa and Portugal, and ship to more than 40 countries. The car boot is now a flagship. The attitude hasn't changed.

What we stand for

Three rules.

  1. 01

    Design loud.

    Colour is a language. We'd rather be remembered than blend in — every piece has to earn its place in your rotation.

  2. 02

    Make it last.

    Heavyweight fabrics, reinforced seams, honest materials. If it can't survive two years of daily wear, it doesn't ship.

  3. 03

    Move together.

    We pay fair, produce in small batches and put 5% of every drop into youth creative programmes across Lagos.
